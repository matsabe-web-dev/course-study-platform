What Our Graduates Say

Quick Questions

Do I need any prior experience to start?

No. Our Web Development and UI/UX tracks are designed for complete beginners. Our Data Analytics track assumes basic spreadsheet comfort, nothing more.

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes. Our self-paced format is built specifically for working professionals, and evening cohort sessions are available for most tracks.

Do you help with job placement after graduation?

Every graduate gets portfolio review, mock interview practice, and access to our employer network before and after graduation.

See the full FAQs

Do you offer any free introductory content?

Yes - check our blog for free tutorials, or try a challenge in the Student Playground before enrolling.

Is there a payment plan available?

Yes. Most tracks can be split into monthly installments. See our pricing page for full details.

What happens if I fall behind in a cohort?

Your mentor will work with you one-on-one to catch up. Self-paced learners can also switch tracks without penalty if a cohort schedule isn't working for them.
