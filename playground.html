Student Playground

Sharpen your skills between lessons with bite-sized challenges, organized by track and difficulty. Pick one, work through it at your own pace, and bring questions to your next mentor session.

Web Development Challenges

  • Build a Responsive Navbar

    Practice semantic HTML and accessible navigation patterns by building a multi-page site header from scratch.

    Level: Beginner

  • Accessible Signup Form

    Build a fully labeled, keyboard-navigable signup form with proper validation states.

    Level: Beginner

  • Recreate a Pricing Table

    Given a screenshot of a pricing page, rebuild the structure using semantic HTML tables or a definition list, whichever fits best.

    Level: Intermediate

Data Analytics Challenges

  • Clean a Messy Dataset

    Given a raw CSV of sales data, identify and fix formatting errors, duplicates, and missing values.

    Level: Intermediate

  • Build a Summary Table

    Turn a raw dataset into a clear, well-labeled summary table someone could present to a manager.

    Level: Beginner

UI/UX Design Challenges

  • Redesign a Signup Form

    Take a cluttered, inaccessible signup form and redesign it for clarity, accessibility, and visual hierarchy.

    Level: Beginner

  • Audit a Real Website's Accessibility

    Pick any public website and write a short report on three accessibility issues you find, with suggested fixes.

    Level: Intermediate
