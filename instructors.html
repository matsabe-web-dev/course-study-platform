Meet Our Instructors

Learn From People Doing the Work — Not Teaching From a Textbook. Every instructor at Dilse Code Academy is currently working in the field they teach — building the products, running the systems, and solving the problems you'll be trained on. You're not learning theory that's been passed down for a decade; you're learning what's actually being used in production right now, from people who use it every day.

  • David Mwangi

    Lead Web Development Instructor

    David has spent eight years building web applications for fintech and logistics companies across East Africa. He joined Dilse Code Academy after mentoring junior developers informally for years and realizing he wanted to teach full-time.

  • Grace Achieng

    Data Analytics Instructor

    Grace works as a data analyst for a Nairobi-based agricultural tech company, where she builds dashboards used by teams across four countries. She's passionate about making data genuinely accessible to people who've never opened a spreadsheet with confidence before.

  • Kevin Otieno

    UI/UX Design Instructor

    Kevin has designed interfaces for both early-stage startups and established Kenyan banks. He believes good design is invisible — and spends most of his classes teaching students to notice the small details that make an interface feel effortless.

  • Amina Yusuf

    Advanced JavaScript & API Integration Instructor

    Amina is a senior backend engineer who's built and maintained production APIs serving millions of requests. She teaches the Advanced track specifically because she remembers how confusing asynchronous JavaScript felt the first time — and wants to make that click faster for her students.

  • Brian Kiptoo

    Cybersecurity & DevOps Instructor

    Brian spent six years securing infrastructure and automating deployments for banks and telecoms across the region before moving into teaching. He treats every class like an incident response drill — practical, calm, and focused on what actually breaks in production.

  • Lydia Mutiso

    Mobile App Development Instructor

    Lydia has shipped apps on both iOS and Android for startups building for the East African market, including two fintech products currently live on the Play Store. She joined Dilse Code Academy to close the gap she kept seeing between "can write code" and "can ship an app real people use."
