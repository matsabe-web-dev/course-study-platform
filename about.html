About Dilse Code Academy

We started this academy because we believe great engineers, analysts, and designers aren't born — they're trained, mentored, and given real problems to solve.

Our Story

Dilse Code Academy began with a simple observation: too many training programs teach theory without ever putting students in front of a real, messy, imperfect project. We wanted to build something different — a place where every lesson leads to something you can actually build, break, fix, and eventually show an employer.

We started with a single Web Development cohort of twelve students meeting in a shared workspace in Nairobi. Today, we've trained hundreds of graduates across three tracks, but the core promise hasn't changed: small classes, real mentorship, and projects that matter.

Our Teaching Philosophy

We believe people learn best by building, not by memorizing. Every module in every track is structured around a project you'll actually finish — not a quiz you'll forget by next week. Mistakes are part of the process here; some of the most valuable lessons come from debugging your own code at 11pm and finally understanding why it broke.

Our Values

  • Mentorship Over Lecture

    You'll spend more time working alongside a mentor than sitting through a lecture. Real feedback, given often, beats a monologue delivered once.

  • Projects Over Certificates

    A certificate tells someone you finished a course. A portfolio tells them what you can actually do. We optimize for the second one.

  • Community Over Competition

    Cohorts succeed together. We build in group critique sessions, peer code reviews, and study groups because the best developers rarely work in isolation.
